# PCART Internal Launch Communications Policy

Version: **v1**
Date: **2026-09-20**
Status: **POLICY CLOSED (internal communications policy only)**

## 0. What this is and is not

This is an **internal communications policy**. It is **NOT legal advice and NOT legal or compliance approval**, and it does not record any review by legal counsel. **External legal review, if the owner requires it, remains a separate owner-side prerequisite** that this repository cannot satisfy or fabricate.

## 1. Launch-mechanism framing

- Describe the CCA/LBP launch as a **public onchain token sale/auction**.
- **Do not claim it is a "no sale"**, and do not imply a legal distinction that has not been reviewed.
- Do not describe it as a private presale, a fixed-price presale, or an airdrop.

## 2. Prohibited claims

- **No guaranteed return, profit, price, valuation, yield, or fundraising-success claims** of any kind, explicit or implied.
- No passive-income, dividend, or revenue-share framing for token holders.
- No projections presented as expectations. Frozen planning inputs (floor-FDV guardrail, graduation minimum) are internal planning figures, never public promises.

## 3. Robinhood brand boundary

- **Do not imply Robinhood approved, endorsed, listed, sponsored, or partnered with PCART** unless such a relationship is independently documented later.
- **Clearly distinguish Robinhood Chain** (a public EVM network PEPECARTEL deploys to permissionlessly) **from listing or trading on any Robinhood brokerage or app product.** Deploying on Robinhood Chain is not a listing.

## 4. LIVE announcement gate

**Do not announce PCART as LIVE** until all of the following are complete and evidenced:

1. verified mainnet deployment;
2. managed finality;
3. source verification;
4. official contract registration in CARTEL HQ;
5. independently verified liquidity/trading readiness.

Until then, communications may describe intent, progress and schedule only.

## 5. Verification vocabulary

Keep CARTEL HQ's existing terms separate and never collapse them into a safety or endorsement claim: Project Authenticated, Official Telegram Connected, Official Contract Registered, Platform Reviewed. None of them means the token is safe, audited, endorsed, or guaranteed.
